How banks create money

Like Lala the goldsmith, a bank lends out money it is holding for others. Leela deposits ₹100 in the only bank in the country. The bank keeps the cash reserve the RBI requires and lends the rest; the loan is spent and comes back as a new deposit, and the bank lends again. Run the rounds, measure the money multiplier, then pull the RBI’s levers, and break the multiplier. The numbers follow NCERT Introductory Macroeconomics, Chapter 3 (Tables 3.1–3.3).
