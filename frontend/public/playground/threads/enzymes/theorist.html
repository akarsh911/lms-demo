Enzyme kinetics

Every dot is a molecule. Substrates wander about at random; when one drifts into an enzyme’s active site it binds, and the enzyme either turns it into product or lets it go. The rate is simply the products counted each second, and the page records it whenever the pond has settled. Add substrate until every enzyme is busy, fit Vmax and Km from your own points, then heat the enzymes, change the pH, and set a competitive inhibitor against a non-competitive one.
