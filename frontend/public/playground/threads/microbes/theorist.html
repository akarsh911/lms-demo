Microbes in human welfare

Three microbial processes, each computed from a population of microbes that grows, feeds and runs out of food. Ferment a jar of milk into curd, run a sewage aeration tank until the water is clean enough to release, and fill a biogas digester with methane from cattle dung. The temperature law that sets how fast each microbe works is a slider you can change yourself — measure what it does, and find where the process breaks. NCERT Class 12 Biology, Chapter 8.
