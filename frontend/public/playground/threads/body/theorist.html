The nerve impulse

A length of axon whose membrane is live. Its sodium and potassium channels open and close by voltage, following the equations Hodgkin and Huxley measured on the squid giant axon, and the current spreads along the fibre from patch to patch. Nothing here is a drawn spike: every impulse happens because the channels opened. Find the threshold, fire it twice, change the ions, wrap it in myelin, and follow the impulse across a synapse.
