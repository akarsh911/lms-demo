Waves, tides and currents

Three models of moving ocean water. Tides: the Moon and the Sun each stretch the ocean, and you set the law that says how strongly; a harbour gauge measures what the sea does. Waves: the wind makes a wave, and linear wave theory carries it to a beach, where you follow the water itself. Currents: the wind drives an ocean basin and the Coriolis force turns it; the page solves for the flow.
