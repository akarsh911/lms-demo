The cardiac cycle, measured

This left heart is not an animation. Each instant the model works out the pressure in the atrium and the ventricle from how full they are and how strongly their muscle is contracting. A valve opens only when the pressure behind it is higher than the pressure in front. The Wiggers diagram, the pressure–volume loop, the ECG and “lub-dub” are all read from the model as it runs. Set the heart yourself, freeze any moment, and push it until it fails.
