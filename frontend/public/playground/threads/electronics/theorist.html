The p–n junction

Silicon barely conducts — until a few atoms in a million are swapped for arsenic or boron. Dope it and watch the electrons and holes balance by nₑnₕ = nᵢ²; heat it until the doping stops mattering. Make half the wafer n-type and watch carriers diffuse, leave charged ions behind and build the barrier that stops them. Then put a diode in a circuit, draw its I–V curve point by point, and use it to turn 50 Hz ac into dc.
