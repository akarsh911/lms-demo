Kepler, measured

Newton’s gravity falls off as 1/r². Here you choose the law: F ∝ 1/rⁿ, any n from −1 to 4. Launch a planet, then measure what Kepler measured — the area it sweeps, whether its orbit closes, how its period grows with its size — and find out which of his laws belong to gravity, and which belong to every force that points at the Sun.
