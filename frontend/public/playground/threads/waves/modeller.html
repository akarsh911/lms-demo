Sound waves

A loudspeaker pushes and pulls on the air in a tube. Each thin slice of air is joined to the next like a spring, and the page works out how every slice moves, many times a frame. Watch the squeezes (compressions) and stretches (rarefactions) travel while the air itself stays put, hear what frequency and amplitude do to the note, measure speed = frequency × wavelength, and time an echo off a wall.
