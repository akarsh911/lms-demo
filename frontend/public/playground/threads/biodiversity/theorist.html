Species and area

CBSE Class 12 Biology, Ch 13 “Biodiversity and its Conservation”. A patch of forest is populated, one plant at a time, with 45 species in the numbers a real community would have — a few common, most rare. Grow the sample quadrat and watch species pile up, then level off; measure the slope Z of log S against log A on the model's own points. Then leave the one forest and sample whole, separate continents instead — the same law, but far steeper — until you make the continents share their species and the steepness collapses.
