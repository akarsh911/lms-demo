Production and costs

A farmer has a fixed field and hires workers one at a time. The output comes from NCERT’s own production table (Table 3.1), or from a law you draw yourself. Measure total, average and marginal product, find where diminishing returns begin, and try to break the law. Then scale both inputs up to test returns to scale, and read the short-run cost curves (Table 3.3) to see marginal cost cut AVC and AC at their lowest points. From NCERT Introductory Microeconomics, Chapter 3.
