Dynamics of population

This village has a thousand people, and the model keeps track of every one of them: each year it decides who is born, who dies and who moves away. You set three things only: how many children a woman has in her lifetime (the fertility rate), how long people live (life expectancy), and how many people move in or out. Watch the village, its population pyramid and its birth and death rates change, year by year.
