Equilibrium and rate

Two questions about every reaction: how far does it go, and how fast? On the first tab a reversible reaction runs forward and backward at the rates its own concentrations set, and settles wherever those rates become equal. Add a gas, squeeze the vessel or heat it, and watch it settle again. On the second, molecules react only when a collision carries enough energy. Measure the rate law, the half-life and the activation energy from your own runs.
