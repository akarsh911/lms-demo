Conic sections

Spin a line about an axis and you get a double cone. Cut it with a plane: tilt the plane and the cut is a circle, an ellipse, a parabola or a hyperbola. Every curve on this page is the true intersection of the plane with the cone. Find its foci with Dandelin’s spheres, measure the ratio that makes all four curves one family, and break the cut by passing it through the vertex.
