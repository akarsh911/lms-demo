Matrices move the plane

A 2 × 2 matrix is a machine that moves every point of the plane: its first column is where (1, 0) goes, its second where (0, 1) goes. Drag the columns and watch the grid stretch, turn and flip; measure the area of what the unit square becomes and compare it with the determinant. Then solve AX = B by hunting for the one point X that lands on B, make the determinant zero and watch the hunt fail, and find out why AB and BA are different machines.
