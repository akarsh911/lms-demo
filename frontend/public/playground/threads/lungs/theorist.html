Breathing and exchange of gases

A chest that breathes by pressure: pull the diaphragm down and the lungs grow, the pressure inside falls below the air outside, and air flows in until they match. A spirometer records every breath. From the air you move, the model works out the partial pressures of O₂ and CO₂ in the alveoli, lets them diffuse into the blood along a capillary, and loads haemoglobin on its sigmoid curve. At the muscle the curve shifts with CO₂, H⁺ and heat. Measure what is unloaded, then break the lungs and see what fails.
