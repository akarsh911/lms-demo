Orienting yourself

Two points on a grid are all it takes. Drag A and B to any grid crossing and the model does the rest: their distance, worked out the same way you would with a set square and Pythagoras; a third point that divides the segment in a ratio you choose; and a fourth point, tested for whether it sits on the very same line. Nothing here is looked up — every number below is computed from where you put the points.
