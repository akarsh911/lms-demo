Work, energy and power

Three experiments from NCERT Class 11 Physics, Chapter 5. Drive a car into a spring whose force law you choose, and measure the work as the area under its force–displacement graph. Collide carts and pucks through a contact spring that gives back only part of its energy, and keep a ledger of momentum and kinetic energy. Whirl a bob on a string and find the speed below which the string goes slack. Every number is measured from an integrated motion, not looked up.
