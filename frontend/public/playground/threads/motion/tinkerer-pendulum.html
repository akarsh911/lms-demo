The swinging pendulum

Galileo watched a lamp swing in a church and timed it with his own pulse. Here you hang two pendulums, Twin A and Twin B, from the same beam. Change one thing about Twin B — its string, its bob, or how far you pull it — release them together, and watch whether they keep the same beat or drift apart. Time is yours: pause it, slow it down, or step through a swing at a time.
