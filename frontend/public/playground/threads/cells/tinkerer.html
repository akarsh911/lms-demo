Zoom into life

An elephant is enormous and an ant is tiny. Are the elephant’s cells enormous too? Decide before you touch anything. Then set up a microscope the way Robert Hooke did in 1665, focus it, and measure real cells against a scale bar. Zoom from a whole body down to one cell, match cells to their jobs, and watch yeast — too small to see — make dough rise.
