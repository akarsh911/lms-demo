Logarithms

2 × 2 × 2 = 8, so the logarithm of 8 to the base 2 is 3: it counts how many times you multiply by the base. Hop along two number lines to count, multiply on a slide rule by adding lengths, and read earthquakes, acidity, sound and music on scales where equal steps mean equal ratios. This chapter is from CBSE’s optional Mathematics at Advanced Level book for Class 9: extra reach for the curious, and a powerful idea to meet early.
