Slope fields

A differential equation dy/dx = F(x, y) does not tell you a curve; it tells you the slope of every curve at every point. Draw those slopes as a field, then launch a solution from any point by stepping along it, with Euler's method or the finer RK4, and check each curve against the book's exact solution. Halve the step and measure what happens to the error. Then zoom in on piecewise functions: a smooth join turns straight, but a corner stays a corner.
