Oceans and life

Dive from the continental shelf to the bottom of a trench. The sea floor follows the depths and slopes in the book; the temperature, saltiness and light at every depth are worked out from how the sea is heated from above and mixed by the wind, how evaporation, rain and rivers change its salt, and how quickly water swallows sunlight. Then find out what makes one sea saltier than another, and let the winds drive the great ocean currents that warm some coasts and chill others. (Anchored on NCERT Class 11 Geography, Chapters 12 and 13, since the Class 9 book’s Part 2 is not yet published.)
