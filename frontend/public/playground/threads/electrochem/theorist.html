The Daniell cell and Nernst

Pick two metals from the electrochemical series and dip each in a solution of its own ions. The voltmeter reads what the Nernst equation gives for your concentrations and temperature. Close the switch and electrons flow through the wire while ions cross the salt bridge; the concentrations change by Faraday’s law and the cell runs down. Oppose it with an outside voltage and it runs backwards. A second tab measures how well solutions conduct.
