Quadrilaterals and area

Stretch a rubber band round four pins of a geoboard. Is it a parallelogram? A rhombus? A square? Its name follows what you can measure: the sides, the angles and the two diagonals. Then slide the top of a parallelogram sideways, cut it and move the piece, and see what happens to its area.
