Mirrors, lenses and shadows

Light travels in straight lines, and everything on this bench follows from that. Hold a ball in front of a lamp and its shadow is cast by the rays it blocks. Shine a thin beam at a mirror and measure the angles. Then move a candle in front of curved mirrors and lenses and watch where the traced rays meet.
