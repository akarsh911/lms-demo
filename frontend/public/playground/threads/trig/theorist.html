The unit circle

Walk a point round a circle of radius 1. The distance it walks is the angle in radians; its height is the sine and its shadow on the axis is the cosine, and as it goes round again and again it draws their graphs. Measure the periods, speed the point up, add two angles and catch the tempting wrong formula — then cut sine down to a branch that can be undone, and reflect it in y = x.
