Physical or chemical?

Melt, tear, rust, burn: watch the particles underneath each change. If they are the same particles, just rearranged, it is a physical change. If new particles appear, it is a chemical change — and that is a different question from whether the change can be undone. Then heat iron and sulfur together and turn a mixture into a compound.
