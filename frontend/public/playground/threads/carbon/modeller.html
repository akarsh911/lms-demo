Carbon and its compounds

Carbon makes four bonds, no more and no fewer. Build molecules one carbon at a time: the model refuses a fifth bond, fills every spare bond with hydrogen, and names what you made by the rules in NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 4 (and the IUPAC numbering you will meet in Class 11). It counts how many different molecules share one formula, lines up the homologous series, and runs the chapter’s reactions. Then drop soap into oily water and watch micelles form.
