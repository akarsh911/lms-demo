Surface area and volume

Turn a cylinder, a cone or a sphere, then unfold it: its surface area is the area of the flat net. Fill a cone with water and pour it into the cylinder it fits in. Join two solids, as the book’s toys and rockets are joined, and watch which faces disappear from the surface while the volumes simply add. Then sweep a sector of a circle and cut off its segment. NCERT Class 10, Chapter 12, Surface Areas and Volumes, and Chapter 11, Areas Related to Circles.
