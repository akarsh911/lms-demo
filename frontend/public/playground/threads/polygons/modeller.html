Circles and tangents

NCERT Class 10, Chapter 10. A line can cut a circle twice, touch it once, or miss it. Slide a line across a circle, or turn it about a point, and watch its two crossing points close in and merge into a tangent. Measure the angle the tangent makes with the radius. Then draw the tangents from a point outside the circle and compare their lengths.
