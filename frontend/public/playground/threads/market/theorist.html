Market equilibrium, measured

Write the laws yourself: qD = a − b·p and qS = c + d·p. The market is a crowd of buyers and sellers, one lot each, lined up by how much they value the good. Let the price find the crossing, then fix it by law — a ceiling or a floor — and measure what the textbook triangle says is lost, and what is really lost. Finally, meet one buyer and watch her choices draw a demand curve.
