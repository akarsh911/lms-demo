The immune response

Acquired immunity is specific and has memory. This body is a clonal model: a pathogen multiplies, innate defences remove some of it, helper T cells expand and help B cells expand, B cells secrete antibodies, and a share of the expanded cells stays behind as memory. Infect it, vaccinate it or give it ready-made antibodies, and measure each response. Then change the rules (no memory, fewer helper T cells) and see what breaks.
