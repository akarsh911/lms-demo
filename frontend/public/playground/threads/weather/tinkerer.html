Winds, storms and cyclones

Air moves from high pressure to low pressure. Everything on this page follows from that, and from the fact that land heats up and cools down much faster than the sea. Heat the coast at Mumbai and watch a breeze begin. Grow a cyclone over a warm Bay of Bengal and steer it onto the east coast. Then move a town round India and see what decides its climate.
