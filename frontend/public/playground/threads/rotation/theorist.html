Spinning and rolling

Three experiments from NCERT Class 11 Physics, Chapter 6. A skater (the book’s girl on a swivel chair) pulls her arms in while no outside torque acts, so L = Iω must stay fixed: watch what that does to her speed and her energy, then add friction and break it. Balance a see-saw by the principle of moments, and find that the plank’s own weight has a moment too. Then race a ring, a disc and a sphere down a slope: every motion here is integrated step by step from the torques on it. The race is a stretch: rolling down a slope was trimmed from the current chapter, and it uses nothing more than the moments of inertia in its Table 6.1.
