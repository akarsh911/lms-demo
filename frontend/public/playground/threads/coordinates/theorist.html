Straight lines

Two rungs up from Orienting Yourself: the same distance formula, run on points that now define a whole line rather than a segment. Drag either point on Line 1 and every form of its equation is re-derived on the spot — slope, point-slope, two-point, slope-intercept, general and normal. Add a second line to measure the angle between them, and a loose point to measure its distance from Line 1. Then push Line 1 to vertical and watch which forms survive.
