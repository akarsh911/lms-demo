Traditional water harvesting, from the chapter
Tankas, Rajasthan — underground rooftop tanks; one at Phalodi was 6.1 m deep, 4.27 m long, 2.44 m wide.
Khadins, Jaisalmer, and johads, elsewhere in Rajasthan — fields turned into rain-fed storage.
Guls / kuls, Western Himalayas — diversion channels for hill agriculture.
Bamboo drip irrigation, Meghalaya, 200 years old — 18–20 litres a minute enters the pipes, and 20–80 drops a minute reach the plant.
Tamil Nadu was the first state to make rooftop rainwater harvesting compulsory. In Shillong, near the wettest places on Earth, rooftop harvesting still supplies 15–25% of a household's water.
