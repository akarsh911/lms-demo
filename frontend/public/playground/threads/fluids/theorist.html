Fluids at rest and in motion

Five experiments from NCERT Class 11 Physics, Chapter 9. Probe the pressure in vessels of different shapes, where it is built up layer by layer from dP/dy = −ρg. Push a hydraulic lift, and spoil it with an air bubble. Narrow a pipe and watch continuity and Bernoulli set the gauge levels. Drop balls through oil and time them between two marks: the drag grows with speed until the ball stops speeding up, and you fit how its terminal speed depends on its radius, and see where Stokes’ law gives way. Then dip a glass tube and watch the liquid climb until surface tension and weight balance.
