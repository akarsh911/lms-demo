Mendel’s garden

CBSE Class 10 · Ch 8 Heredity · ICSE Class 10 Biology: genetics

Every plant in this garden grows from one egg cell and one pollen grain. Which copy of each gene goes into the egg or the pollen is decided by chance, like tossing a coin, and the model tosses it for every plant. Grow a few plants, then a thousand, and see which ratios appear. On the syllabus: NCERT Class 10, Chapter 8 Heredity.
