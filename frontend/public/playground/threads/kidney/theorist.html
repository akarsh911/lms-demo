The nephron at work

Follow the plasma through one nephron, standing for the two million in your kidneys. The glomerulus filters only where blood pressure beats the pull of the plasma proteins. The PCT takes back most of the filtrate. The loop of Henle builds the medulla's salty gradient, one turn at a time. The collecting duct keeps as much water as ADH lets it. Every number here comes from the model as it runs. Change the hormones, the loop and the blood, and see what reaches the bladder.
