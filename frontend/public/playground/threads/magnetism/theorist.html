Induction and AC

Faraday’s law says the emf is the rate of change of flux linkage, ε = −N dΦ/dt. Here the flux of a real bar magnet through a coil is worked out from the Biot–Savart law, and the emf, the current and the push-back on the magnet follow from it as you drag. Measure what the law predicts, then change its sign and watch energy conservation fail. After that, spin a generator coil and tune a series LCR circuit.
