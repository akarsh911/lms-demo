Double circulation

A heart with four chambers pumps blood round two loops: one through the lungs and one through the body. Every dot is 5 mL of blood, about twenty-five billion red cells. Each dot moves only when the heart pushes it and a valve lets it through, and it carries its own oxygen. Tag one red cell and follow it. Then run, and see what a fish or a frog does differently.
