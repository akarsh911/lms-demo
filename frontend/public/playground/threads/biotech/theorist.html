Cut, copy and sort DNA

The tools of recombinant DNA, working on real sequences. A restriction enzyme scans the letters for its palindrome and cuts where it finds it; ligase joins ends only when their single-stranded tails pair; E. coli that took up a plasmid live on ampicillin; a gel sorts the pieces by size; and PCR copies a gene cycle by cycle. The plasmid is laid out like pBR322 (its sites, ori, ampR and tetR where the book’s map puts them), but its letters are invented.
