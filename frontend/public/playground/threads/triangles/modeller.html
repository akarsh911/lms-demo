Congruence theorems

Two triangles are congruent when every one of their six parts (three sides, three angles) matches. You never need to check all six — some smaller sets of given parts pin a triangle down completely (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS), and one nearly-there set (SSA, two sides and an angle not between them) does not. Build a triangle from each set of given parts and see for yourself, by construction and by measurement.
