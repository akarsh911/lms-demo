Global winds and the monsoon

Nothing here is drawn from a diagram in the book. Each parcel of air is pushed from high pressure to low pressure, slowed by friction, and carried round on a spinning sphere; its path is integrated step by step (fourth-order Runge–Kutta). The pressure belts, the trade winds and the westerlies come out of that. Measure the Coriolis turning yourself, change the Earth's spin, then heat India and watch the Inter Tropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ) bring the south-west monsoon.
