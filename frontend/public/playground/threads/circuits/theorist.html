Kirchhoff, measured

Every current on this board comes from Kirchhoff’s two rules, solved for whatever you wire: the currents into a junction equal the currents out, and the potential changes round any closed loop add to zero. Give a cell its own emf and internal resistance and measure them back off your graph. Walk a loop and add up the steps. Balance a Wheatstone bridge by hand. Then push the model: short a cell, heat a filament until Ohm’s law fails, and look inside a wire to see how slowly the electrons actually move.
