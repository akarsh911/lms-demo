Hormones of the menstrual cycle

Four hormones, two glands and a set of feedback rules. Nothing below is a drawn curve: the pituitary releases FSH and LH, the follicle and the corpus luteum release oestrogen (NCERT writes estrogens) and progesterone, and each level feeds back on the others, day by day. The cycle's length, the day of ovulation and the days of menstruation are what the rules produce. Change a rule and measure what happens. The second tab follows a pollen tube into an embryo sac and counts the chromosomes of every nucleus that fuses.
