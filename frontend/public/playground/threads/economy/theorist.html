Circular flow and the multiplier

A two-sector economy: households sell their work to firms and spend what they earn on the firms’ goods. Some income is saved; firms invest. Measure one year’s GDP at three points of the flow, then change the investment and watch the economy spend its way, round by round, to a new income. Every round is computed: C = C̄ + c·Y, and income is what was spent.
