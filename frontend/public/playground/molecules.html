Pick atoms and bond them, or load one from the shelf. The Lewis structure, the VSEPR shape, the bond angles, the hybridisation and the dipole are all computed from the atoms you placed — electron pairs on a sphere push each other apart, and the shape is wherever they settle.

How this model is calibrated

Measured chemistry, fixed and not tunable here: electronegativities (Pauling), covalent radii, valence electron counts, and ethane’s torsional barrier (≈12.5 kJ/mol, a standard measured value).
This model’s own fitted constants, chosen so the shapes match the textbook and shown wherever they matter: the lone-pair repulsion strengths λ = 1.28 (a lone pair pushing a bond pair) and Λ = 1.2 for ordinary four-region atoms, Λ = 2.0 for five- and six-region atoms with two or more lone pairs; the multiple-bond bulk 1.20 per extra bond order, fitted to ethene’s 117° and 121.5°; and the repulsion law’s power p = 3.
