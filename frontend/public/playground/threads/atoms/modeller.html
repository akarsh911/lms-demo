Journey inside the atom

Geiger and Marsden fired alpha particles at gold foil so thin that light shone through it. Here every alpha particle is pushed by Coulomb’s law from the gold atom’s positive charge, and its path is worked out step by step. Build the atom Thomson’s way or Rutherford’s way, fire, and count the flashes. Then build atoms yourself, shell by shell, the way NCERT Class 9 Chapter 8 does.
