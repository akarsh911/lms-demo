Chemical coordination

Each gland here is a compartment with its own half-life: the hypothalamus releases into the pituitary, the pituitary releases into the thyroid, and the thyroid's own hormone loops back to slow both of them down — negative feedback, not a script. Starve the model of the iodine that hormone is built from and watch what the feedback alone does to TSH. A second tool runs insulin and glucagon the same way to hold blood glucose steady, and shows what breaks when the beta cells that make insulin stop.
