Heights and distances

Drag a right triangle. Make it bigger, make it steeper, and watch sin A, cos A and tan A — on the triangle, on a circle of radius 1 and on their graphs at once. Then take a clinometer outside: sight the top of a tower, a chimney, a TV tower you cannot walk to, and two ships from a lighthouse, and let the ratios measure what a tape cannot.
