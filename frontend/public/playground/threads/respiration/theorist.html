Respiration in cells

Every bead is a carbon atom, coloured by where it sat in the glucose. Enzymes pass molecules down glycolysis in the cytoplasm, into the mitochondrion, round the Krebs cycle and out as CO₂, but only while they have NAD⁺ to take the hydrogen. NADH and FADH₂ feed the electron transport system, which pumps protons, and ATP synthase lets them back for ATP. Nothing is quoted: the ATP, the O₂ and the CO₂ are counted as the model runs. Feed it, cut its oxygen, change the rules.
