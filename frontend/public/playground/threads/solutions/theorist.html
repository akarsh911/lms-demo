Solutions and colligative properties

A liquid’s vapour pressure is molecules leaving its surface. Here they leave one at a time, at random, and come back when they hit the surface again. Put a solute in and it takes some of the places at the surface, so fewer solvent molecules can leave. Measure that, and Raoult’s law, the higher boiling point, the lower freezing point and the rise in an osmosis tube all follow from the same counting. Then change how unlike molecules hold each other, and watch the law bend.
