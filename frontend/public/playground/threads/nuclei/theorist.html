Nuclei

Every nucleus is lighter than the protons and neutrons it is made of — the missing mass is the energy holding it together. Here that energy comes from one nuclear mass formula, with its surface and Coulomb terms left as sliders for you to set. Watch the binding energy per nucleon rise, peak near iron and fall again; split a heavy nucleus or join light ones and read the energy straight off the same curve; and watch a few hundred nuclei decide, one at a time and at random, when to decay.
