Conditional probability and Bayes

Ten thousand people, each one drawn by the model: who has a rare disease, and what their test says. Keep only the people who tested positive and count how many of them are ill. That count is P(ill | positive); Bayes’ theorem is what the count comes to. Set the disease, the test and the factory yourself, test twice, then find out on two dice what “independent” really means.
