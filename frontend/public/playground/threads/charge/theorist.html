Fields and potential

Place point charges on a sheet. The field at every point is the vector sum of Coulomb’s law for each charge, and the potential is the sum of kq/r; the field lines and equipotentials you see are traced from those sums, not drawn. Wrap a Gaussian surface round the charges and the page integrates the flux. Then change the exponent in Coulomb’s law and find out which results were really about the inverse square.
