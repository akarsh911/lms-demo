Vectors in space

Two arrows in a space you can turn. Add them tip to tail, drop one onto the other to project it, and read the cross product as the area of the parallelogram they span, its three components as the areas of that parallelogram’s shadows. Then take two lines in space and hunt, by hand, for the shortest segment between them. Drag empty space to turn the view; drag an arrow’s tip to move it level with the floor.
