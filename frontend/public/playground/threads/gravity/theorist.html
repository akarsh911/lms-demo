Why the Moon doesn’t fall

Newton’s thought experiment: a cannon on a mountain above the air, fired horizontally, faster each time. Each path is integrated numerically (velocity Verlet) under a = −GM r̂/r² with the Earth’s real GM and radius, not drawn from a formula. Find the speed at which the fall matches the curve of the Earth, v = √(GM/r), and the speed at which the ball never returns, v = √(2GM/r). Then test Newton’s own check that the Moon falls by the same law as an apple.
