Rays and waves

Two views of light. As rays: build a telescope and a compound microscope from two thin lenses, trace the light through them, and measure the magnifying power yourself. As waves: add the waves from two slits, point by point, measure the fringe width, and push the set-up until λD/d stops being true. Then turn polaroids and test Malus’s law.
