Maps and directions

Manu keeps track of directions by facing the rising Sun. Turn him round until he faces sunrise, and find out which way his hands point. Then help an ant crawl along the dotted lines of a zoo garden — count the centimetres and the directions it takes to reach the laddoos, the bread and the apple, and see if you can find a shorter way than the one the book suggests.
