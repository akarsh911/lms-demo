Bits, gates and memory

A computer keeps everything as bits. Switch bits on and off to count, convert what you have made to octal and hexadecimal the way the book does, and add two numbers through a chain of full adders, watching the carries ripple. Then wire logic gates yourself: the truth table is worked out from your wiring, so it can prove De Morgan's law, build XOR from simpler gates, and grow into the half and full adders the counter runs on.
