Transforming sentences

“As he was tired, he slept.” “Being tired, he slept.” “He was tired, so he slept.” Three structures — simple, compound, complex — and one meaning. Choose a sentence, or type your own; the page finds its clauses, rewrites it every way the grammar allows, and then proves each rewrite says the same thing by reading the facts back out of it.
