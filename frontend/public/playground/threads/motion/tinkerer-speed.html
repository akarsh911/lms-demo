Reading a journey from its graph

This time you don't set the speeds and watch the graph appear — you draw the graph first, by dragging its six points, and the vehicle plays back exactly the journey those points describe. A steep stretch means it covered a lot of distance quickly: fast. A flat stretch means the distance isn't changing at all: stopped. Play the journey at your own pace — pause it, slow it, or step through it minute by minute.
