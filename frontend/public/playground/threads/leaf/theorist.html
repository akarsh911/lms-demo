Light reactions and the Calvin cycle

A thylakoid membrane and the stroma around it, running. Photons drive electrons from water through photosystem II, the cytochrome b₆f complex and photosystem I to NADP⁺; the protons they leave in the lumen turn ATP synthase. The Calvin cycle spends that ATP and NADPH, and its pools of RuBP, PGA and triose phosphate rise and fall from their own rate equations. Set the light, the gases and the machinery, then measure what it costs to fix one CO₂.
