Refraction and the eye

Light bends when it crosses from air into glass, by exactly as much as Snell’s law says. Measure it, then use the same rays to find images with the lens and mirror formulas, squeeze the lens of a model eye, fix a short-sighted and a long-sighted eye with a lens of your own power, and split white light with a prism. NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 9 (Light: Reflection and Refraction) and Chapter 10 (The Human Eye and the Colourful World).
