The atmosphere, layer by layer

Ride a weather balloon up from the beach at Mumbai. The temperature and the pressure are worked out from two rules as you climb: the air gets colder at a steady rate in the lowest layer, and every layer of air is pressed down by the weight of all the air above it. Then put those rules to work: build a town's climate from its latitude, its height, its distance from the sea and the monsoon winds, and read it as a climograph.
