Similar triangles

Two figures are similar when they have the same shape, whatever their size: equal angles, and every side scaled by the same number. Slide a line parallel to one side of a triangle and measure how it cuts the other two sides (Thales’ basic proportionality theorem). Enlarge a triangle from a lamp, then pull it out of shape and see which similarity test fails. Then use similarity to find a shadow. NCERT Class 10, Chapter 6, Triangles.
