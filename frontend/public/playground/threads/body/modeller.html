Tissues in action

A joint is two bones meeting: what decides whether it swings like a shoulder, hinges like a knee, or only spins like your neck is the shape where the bones meet. Build each kind, then try to move the limb every way you can think of — the geometry itself will stop you where a real joint stops you. Watch the ligament (a strap of tissue that only connects bone to bone) and the tendon (which connects the pulling muscle to the bone) work as you move it.

Tissues
◼ bone ◼ cartilage ◼ ligament ◼ tendon & muscle

Ligaments join bone to bone and only stretch tighter; they do not pull. Tendons join muscle to bone: the muscle shortens, the tendon carries the pull.
