A cell in a beaker

A cell’s membrane lets water through but not the salt or sugar dissolved in it. Put a cell into water, into salt solution or into sugar syrup, and watch the water cross, particle by particle. The cell swells, shrinks, bursts — or, if it has a wall, holds firm. Build the graph of what happens from your own runs.
