Round and round

A circle keeps two secrets in this page. Drag a point around its rim and an angle at the centre appears to stay locked to half of it, wherever you put the point — measure it and see. Then cut the same circle into sectors, lay them out alternately, and watch the wobbly strip you get straighten into a rectangle as the sectors get thinner: its width and height are exactly the numbers the area of a circle is built from.
