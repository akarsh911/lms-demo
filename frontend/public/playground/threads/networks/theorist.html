Computer networks

Pick a topology — star, bus, ring or mesh — and click two computers to send a packet between them. The route is found hop by hop by a real routing model over whatever links are up, and the transmission and propagation delay at every hop is computed from the bandwidth, the packet size and the cable length you set, the way CBSE Class 12 Computer Science works them out. Click a link (or, in a star, turn the switch off) to break it and watch what a topology does when part of it fails: some reroute around the damage, and some do not.
