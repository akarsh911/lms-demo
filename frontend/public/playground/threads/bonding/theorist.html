Shapes of molecules

VSEPR says the electron pairs round a central atom push each other as far apart as they can. Here they really do: each pair sits on a sphere, every pair repels every other by a law you set, and the model slides them downhill until the repulsion is least. The shape, the bond angles and the dipole moment are measured from where they come to rest. Drag to turn the molecule; drag a lone pair onto an atom and they swap places.
